List the Employee Name, their Department Name, and the Location Name for all employees.
Ans : SELECT Employees.employee_name, Departments.dept_name, Departments.location_id FROM Employees JOIN Departments ON Employees.dept_id = Departments.dept_id;

2.
Retrieve the Project Name, Client Name, and the Total Hours Worked on that project, but only for projects managed by the 'Consulting' department.
SELECT Projects.project_name, Projects.client_name, SUM(Assignments.hours_worked) FROM Projects JOIN Assignments ON Projects.project_id = Assignments.project_id JOIN Departments ON Projects.dept_id = Departments.dept_id WHERE Departments.dept_name = 'Consulting' GROUP BY Projects.project_name, Projects.client_name;
